I have been a counsellor since 2014 and I started working solely online in 2017 because I believe online counselling is an effective, accessible, progressive, and convenient mode of counselling.

The advantages of online counselling include:

- You can do it from the comfort of your own home or wherever you happen to be
- There is no travel time
- There are no travel or parking costs
- It is useful if you live in a rural location and/or if you have disability access requirements
- It may suit you if you would feel more comfortable communicating online rather than face to face
- You have the option of communicating via the written or spoken word
- It is handy if you travel around or change locations a lot

I provide online counselling via Zoom - either video call or instant messaging; it's also possible to do via secure e-mail.

If you are interested in giving online counselling a go please just e-mail me and I'll send you some further information.

NB There are now a lot of counsellors offering online counselling but please be aware that counsellors that offer online counselling should have done specialist training - I have completed an Online Counselling Skills course with OCST - Online Counselling Services & Training - an approved ACTO [Association for Counselling and Therapy Online] training provider.
